# 📚 Akshara — Learn Indian Languages the Smart Way

Akshara is a Duolingo-style mobile application focused on helping users learn **Indic languages** in a fun, intuitive, and structured manner. It currently supports Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, Kannada, and Malayalam, with more languages coming soon.

---

## 🚀 Features

- 🎯 **Multi-language Support:** Choose from Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, Kannada, and Malayalam.
- 🔤 **Modular Learning System:** Each language comes with custom modules covering letters, words, phrases, stories, and culture.
- 🧠 **Bite-sized Lessons:** Learn in quick, daily 5-minute lessons.
- 🎵 **Cultural Learning:** Songs, proverbs, and stories to understand local nuances.
- 📊 **Progress Tracking:** See how far you’ve come and what’s next.
- 🗣️ **Interactive Quizzes & Conversations:** Practice speaking and listening with real-world examples.
- 📱 **Mobile-First Design:** Clean, user-friendly UI designed for all smartphone sizes.

## 📦 Language Modules

### Telugu
1. అనుబంధ అక్షరాలు – Vowel-Consonant Combinations  
2. పదబంధాలు – Common Word Pairs  
3. సంభాషణ – Conversational Phrases  
4. చిన్న కథలు – Mini Stories  
5. గేయాలు & పద్యాలు – Songs & Poems  

### Hindi
1. स्वर और व्यंजन – Vowels & Consonants  
2. संयुक्ताक्षर – Conjunct Letters  
3. दैनिक बातचीत – Daily Conversations  
4. कहानियाँ – Short Stories  
5. मुहावरे और लोकोक्तियाँ – Idioms & Proverbs  

### Tamil
1. உயிர் எழுத்துக்கள் – Vowels  
2. மெய் எழுத்துக்கள் – Consonants  
3. வாக்கியங்கள் – Sentence Structures  
4. உரையாடல்கள் – Dialogues  
5. பழமொழிகள் – Proverbs  

### Kannada
1. ಸ್ವರ ಮತ್ತು ವ್ಯಂಜನ – Vowels & Consonants  
2. ಸಂಯುಕ್ತಾಕ್ಷರಗಳು – Combined Characters  
3. ಸಂಭಾಷಣೆಗಳು – Daily Dialogue  
4. ಕಥೆಗಳು – Story-based Learning  
5. ಮಾತುಗಳು ಮತ್ತು ನುಡಿಗಟ್ಟುಗಳು – Sayings & Phrases  

### Malayalam
1. സ്വരം & വ്യഞ്ജനം – Vowels & Consonants  
2. സംയുക്താക്ഷരങ്ങൾ – Compound Characters  
3. സംഭാഷണം – Basic Conversations  
4. കഥകൾ – Stories  
5. നാടൻ പാട്ടുകൾ – Folk Songs & Rhymes
